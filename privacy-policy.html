Privacy Policy – Horley Cars Driver

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Horley Cars Driver app handles information when drivers use the app to receive and complete taxi and private-hire bookings.

Information we process

The app may process driver account and login information, driver availability and job status, booking and journey information, and device location/GPS information needed for dispatch and live journey operations.

Location data

Location information may be used to show the driver's position for dispatch, assist with navigation and job allocation, and support the operation of active bookings. Location access depends on the permissions granted on the driver's device.

Booking and job information

The app processes information required to carry out bookings, including pickup and destination information and job status such as assigned, accepted, arrived, passenger on board and completed.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and operate the Horley Cars dispatch service, authenticate drivers, allocate and manage jobs, provide live operational updates, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Sharing of information

Information is used only as necessary to operate the Horley Cars service and its supporting technology providers. We do not sell personal information.

Data security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, security, legal and record-keeping purposes.

Your choices and rights

Drivers can control device permissions, including location permissions, through their device settings. Requests concerning personal information, including access, correction or deletion where applicable, can be made using the contact details below.
